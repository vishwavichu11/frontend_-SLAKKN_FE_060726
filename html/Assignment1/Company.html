About company

Google LLC is a leading global technology company that specializes in internet-related services and products. Founded in 1998 by Larry Page and Sergey Brin, the company is headquartered in Mountain View, California. Google is best known for its powerful search engine, but it also provides a wide range of digital services, including Google Cloud Platform, Gmail, and YouTube. With a mission to make the world's information universally accessible, Google continues to drive innovation worldwide through advanced artificial intelligence and cloud computing solutions.
