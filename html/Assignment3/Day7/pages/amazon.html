About AMAZON

Amazon.com, Inc. is an American multinational technology company focusing on e-commerce, cloud computing, online advertising, digital streaming, and artificial intelligence. It has been referred to as "one of the most influential economic and cultural forces in the world."

Amazon is one of the Big Five American information technology companies, alongside Alphabet (Google), Apple, Meta, and Microsoft. Its subsidiary Amazon Web Services (AWS) is a global leader in cloud computing platforms.

History

Amazon was founded by Jeff Bezos from his garage in Bellevue, Washington, on July 5, 1994. Initially an online marketplace for books, it has expanded to offer a vast variety of products and services, earning the nickname "The Everything Store."

The company went public in May 1997. In 2002, Amazon launched Amazon Web Services (AWS), providing data and website popularity statistics. AWS later expanded to offer cloud computing infrastructure, which quickly became a major revenue driver.

Over the years, Amazon has diversified into digital content with Prime Video and Amazon Music, smart home devices with Echo and Alexa, and logistics innovations worldwide under current CEO Andy Jassy.
