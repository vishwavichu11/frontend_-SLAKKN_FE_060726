About DELOITTE

Deloitte Touche Tohmatsu Limited, commonly referred to as Deloitte, is a multinational professional services network. It is one of the "Big Four" accounting organizations, alongside EY, KPMG, and PwC, and is the largest professional services network in the world by revenue and number of professionals.

Deloitte provides audit, consulting, financial advisory, risk advisory, tax, and legal services to global brands, government entities, and private companies across various industries.

History

Deloitte was founded by William Welch Deloitte in London in 1845. Deloitte was the first person to be appointed an independent auditor of a public company, the Great Western Railway. Over the 19th and 20th centuries, the firm expanded globally through organic growth and strategic mergers.

In 1989, Deloitte Haskins & Sells merged with Touche Ross in the United States to form Deloitte & Touche. In 1993, the international firm was renamed Deloitte Touche Tohmatsu, later shortened to Deloitte for branding purposes.

Today, Deloitte operates in over 150 countries and territories with hundreds of thousands of professionals delivering industry-leading audit, cybersecurity, cloud transformation, and technology consulting solutions.
