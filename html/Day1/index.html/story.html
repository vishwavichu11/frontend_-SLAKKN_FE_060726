Oru mazhai naal la Arjun nu oru paiyan bus stop la ninnu irundhaan. Appo oru chinna kutti naai mazhaila nadungitu irukkuratha paathu, avan atha veetukku kondu poi saapadu kuduthu paathukittaan. Konja naal la antha naai avanoda nalla nanban aayiduchu. Oru naal Arjun thavara oru kaadu vazhiya poitu vazhi theriyama alainjappo, antha naai than munnadi odi sariyana vazhiya kaamichu avanai veetukku serthuchu. Appo Arjun oru vishayam purinjukittaan—namma seyyura oru siru nalladhum oru naal namakkaye periya udhaviya thirumbi varum. ❤️
