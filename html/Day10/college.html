Admission Process

  1. Online Registration: Fill out the application form on the official portal with your personal and academic details.
  2. Document Submission: Upload scanned copies of your academic marksheets (10th/12th), entrance exam scorecard, and passport-size photo.
  3. Application Review: The admissions committee evaluates candidates based on cutoff marks, merit rank, and eligibility criteria.
  4. Counseling & Seat Allotment: Qualified candidates will be called for seat selection and branch confirmation.
  5. Fee Payment & Final Verification: Pay the tuition fee to confirm admission and verify original physical documents at the campus.

Campus Facilities

  • Advanced Research Labs: High-speed internet, AI computing servers, and modern hardware workstations.
  • Central Digital Library: Access to over 50,000 journals, books, e-publications, and quiet study areas.
  • On-Campus Hostels: Separate fully-furnished residential halls for boys and girls with 24/7 security and Wi-Fi.
  • Sports & Athletics Complex: Basketball courts, football turf, indoor gymnasium, and Olympic-size swimming pool.
  • Innovation & Startup Cell: Dedicated incubation center providing mentorship, seed funding, and workspace for student startups.
