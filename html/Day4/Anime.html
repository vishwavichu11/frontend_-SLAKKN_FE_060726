About Solo Leveling

Solo Leveling is a popular web novel and manhwa series that follows the journey of Sung Jin-Woo, a weak hunter who becomes the strongest after a mysterious event. The story is filled with action, adventure, and fantasy elements.

Characters

  • Sung Jin-Woo: The protagonist who starts as the weakest hunter and becomes the strongest.
  • Cha Hae-In: A skilled hunter and one of the main characters in the series.
  • Go Gun-Hee: The chairman of the Hunter's Association.
