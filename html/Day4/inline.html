SOLO LEVELING

_____________________________________________________________________________________________________________________

Solo Leveling, also alternatively translated as Only I Level Up (Korean: 나 혼자만 레벨업) is a Korean fantasy web novel written by Chugong. It was serialized in KakaoPage's digital comic and fiction platform KakaoPage beginning on 25, 2016, and was later published by D&C Media under their Papyrus label since November 4, 2016. The novel has been licensed in English by Yen Press.
