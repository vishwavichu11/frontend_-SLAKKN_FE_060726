About FLIPKART

Flipkart Private Limited is an Indian e-commerce company headquartered in Bengaluru, Karnataka, India. It is one of India's leading e-commerce marketplaces, competing primarily with Amazon's Indian subsidiary and domestic rivals.

Initially focused on online book sales, Flipkart rapidly expanded into other product categories such as consumer electronics, fashion, home essentials, groceries, and lifestyle products. In 2018, U.S.-based retail giant Walmart acquired a 77% controlling stake in Flipkart.

History

Flipkart was founded in October 2007 by Sachin Bansal and Binny Bansal, alumni of the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Delhi. Both had previously worked for Amazon before launching their own venture out of an apartment in Bengaluru.

The company grew rapidly by introducing innovative services tailored to Indian consumers, such as Cash on Delivery (CoD) and dedicated in-house logistics networks (Ekart). Over the years, Flipkart acquired major companies including Myntra, Jabong, and PhonePe to strengthen its market position.

Today, Flipkart continues to drive technological innovation in supply chain management, AI-driven personalization, and fintech solutions across India.
